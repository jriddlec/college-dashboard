Study Guide 3.7: Improper Integrals

An integral is improper when an endpoint is infinite or the integrand is unbounded on the interval.

Type 1: infinite interval

Replace the infinite endpoint with a variable and take a limit: ∫1∞ f(x) dx = limt→∞ ∫1t f(x) dx.

Type 2: discontinuous integrand

Split at the discontinuity and take one-sided limits on each piece.

p-test

∫1∞ 1/xp dx converges exactly when p > 1.

Check yourself

  1. Does ∫1∞ 1/x2 dx converge? (Yes, to 1.)
  2. Does ∫01 1/√x dx converge? (Yes, to 2.)
